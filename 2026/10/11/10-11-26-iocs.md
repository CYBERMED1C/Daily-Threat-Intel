# CYBERMEDIC Daily IOC Collection - 2026-10-11

## Executive summary

Passive deep research reviewed 20 current records from 16 independent organizations. Push Security documented the active Adception malvertising chain, in which a Google advertisement, a Bing redirect, and layered cloaking led macOS users to fake Claude installers and a ClickFix-style command that downloaded a payload from attacker-controlled infrastructure. Seven attacker-controlled lure or payload domains from the original IOC appendix were retained. Current individual ThreatFox records supplied Cobalt Strike, AdaptixC2, and AsyncRAT infrastructure; MalwareBazaar supplied two named Mirai samples; and OpenPhish supplied two exact phishing URLs. BleepingComputer and The Hacker News were used only for discovery. Previously published indicators, compromised legitimate redirect infrastructure, shared hosting without an exact malicious path, unattributed samples, unsupported artifacts, and malformed or non-public values were excluded.
